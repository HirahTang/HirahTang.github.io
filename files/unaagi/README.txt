UNAAGI — Gubra meeting deck, 01.10.2026
Han Tang, Alexander Tong, Wouter Boomsma

HOW TO OPEN
  Double-click unaagi_deck.html, or drag it into any modern browser
  (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox). Nothing to install.
  Keep this folder together — the HTML loads the videos and figures
  from assets/ and renders/ by relative path.

NAVIGATION
  Next slide      right arrow, Page Down, space, or the "Next" button
  Previous slide  left arrow, Page Up, or the "Previous" button
  First / last    Home / End

ANIMATIONS
  Each clip plays automatically when its slide opens and then holds on
  its last frame. Hover over a clip for a "Replay" button.

  Slide 3 is different on purpose: it pauses part-way, on the pure-noise
  state, and shows a blue "Now run the reverse process" button. Click it
  to play the second half. The forward and reverse halves are separate
  processes, and the pause is there so they do not read as one take.

FONTS
  The deck uses IBM Plex from Google Fonts. With no internet it falls
  back to system fonts; the layout and figures are unaffected.

CONTENTS
  unaagi_deck.html            the deck (13 slides)
  assets/                     figures: the benchmark panels, the two
                              correlation plots, the method schematics
  renders/zoom/               one "zoom into the site" clip per position
  renders/animations_geo/     one clip per sampled residue
  renders/method/             the forward / reverse illustration
