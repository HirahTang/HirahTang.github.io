Method

Method

Generating side chains to fit their local environment

The backbone and surrounding structure stay fixed while UNAAGI removes the side chain beyond Cα and rebuilds it from noise.

The model generates atom types, positions and bonds together. Because it works directly with atoms, it can build both canonical and non-canonical side chains without choosing from a predefined list of amino acids.

Benchmark

Testing amino acid substitutions in two peptides

The study tested a set of 40 amino acids, replacing one residue at a time and measuring the change in binding (ΔΔG). Negative values mean stronger binding than the original peptide.

How substitutions affect binding

Red means stronger binding. Blue means weaker binding. Values show the change from the original peptide (ΔΔG in kcal/mol).

We count substitutions with ΔΔG < 0.5 kcal/mol as tolerated, allowing a small loss in binding. We test whether UNAAGI proposes these substitutions without access to the assay results.

In progress

Next steps: scoring specific amino acids and learning from assay data

1 · Conditional diffusion

Scoring a chosen amino acid

We are developing a model that estimates how well a chosen amino acid fits its local atomic environment. This will support scoring both canonical and non-canonical substitutions.

2 · Preference optimisation

Learning from experimental results

We plan to use assay results to favour substitutions with better measured binding. New data will guide model updates and the next round of candidate generation.
